About

I study data engineering at the Faculty of Sciences of Sfax. The part of data work I enjoy most is the unglamorous middle: taking Excel files that were never meant to be analysed, cleaning them into a reliable database, and building something on top that other people can actually use.

This summer at STIP in Sousse I built an absenteeism analytics platform from years of scattered spreadsheets: an automated ETL pipeline, a forecasting model, and an assistant that answers questions in French. Before that I built a data warehouse for airport operations, a Power BI dashboard at LEONI, and AI projects with teams at hackathons.

Outside class I am the secretary of the IEEE SMC student branch at my faculty, where I organise events and keep the chapter running.
